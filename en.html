Privacy Policy — Repava

Updated November 8, 2026

What you log in Repava — food, workouts, weight, sleep, recipes and photos — is stored on your iPhone. Health data from Apple Health never leaves the device. Data is only sent on when a feature needs it: food search, recipe import and iCloud sharing, and — if you say yes — usage statistics, AI recipe interpretation and AI photo recognition. The app has no accounts, no ads and no cross-app tracking.

Data controller

Albert Olsson is responsible for the processing of personal data in Repava. Contact: repavainfo@vaao.se.

Data that stays on your device

Your profile (name, sex, date of birth, height, weight, body fat, goals, activity level, diet, allergies and training settings), food diary, water, weight and body measurements, workouts, recipes, weekly menus, shopping lists, household members and progress photos are stored in the app's local database. We keep no copy.

Apple Health

With your permission, Repava reads workouts, active energy, sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), respiratory rate and sleeping wrist temperature from Apple Health to log your workouts, count calories burned toward your calorie goal, show your heart rate while resting between sets, and show sleep and recovery. Repava on Apple Watch reads heart rate and calories burned during a workout and saves the workouts you do there to Apple Health. Health data stays on the device: it is never sent to us, to analytics or to AI, never stored in iCloud and never used for marketing. You can change access in the iPhone Settings app → Health.

Usage statistics (optional)

If you say yes, Repava stores usage statistics in the app's own database in iCloud, run by Apple: which screens are shown and for how long, which buttons are tapped, roughly where on the screen you tap (never what is shown there), when the app is opened and closed, your sex and age group (for example 25–34, never your date of birth), whether you have Repava+, app version, iOS version, language, phone model, screen size, and a random ID that lets statistics from the same phone be followed over time. iCloud also adds an ID for your account that only applies to Repava. Neither reveals who you are. The statistics never include health data, your name or what you log, and the screen is never recorded. You can change your mind at any time under Profile → Settings → Privacy.

AI recipe interpretation (optional)

When you import a recipe, Repava can let Claude, an AI service from Anthropic, read out the ingredients, amounts and steps and translate the recipe. This only happens if you have said yes. The link, the recipe text from the page, text you pasted, or text read from your screenshots on the phone is then sent — never images, your profile or health data. The text goes via Repava's server at Cloudflare, which doesn't store the content but keeps a random install ID for at most one hour to limit abuse. Anthropic doesn't use the data to train its models and deletes it after a limited time under its terms. If you say no, recipes are interpreted with simpler rules right on the phone.

AI photo recognition (optional)

Under Home you can take or pick a photo of the food you have at home, and Repava suggests what you can eat. This only happens if you have said yes to AI photo recognition. The photo — downsized and without location data — is then sent to Claude from Anthropic, which reads out which foods it shows, and you approve what was found before anything is added. The photo goes via Repava's server at Cloudflare, which doesn't store it. To limit the number of photos, the server counts each day's photos per random install ID and per IP address in hashed form (never the address itself); the counts are deleted after a day. Anthropic doesn't use the photos to train its models and deletes them after a limited time under its terms. Don't photograph people or anything else you don't want to share. You can change your mind at any time under Profile → Settings → Privacy.

Food search and recipe import

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search term or barcode is sent to Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central. The Swedish Food Agency's database is built into the app and searched locally. If you import a recipe, the app fetches the web page, or the Instagram or TikTok link, that you enter yourself. As with all internet traffic, these services see your IP address.

Sharing via iCloud

If you share recipes, weekly menus, shopping lists or household members (name and age), they are stored in your own iCloud account and shared with the people you invite. We have no access to your private iCloud data. If you suggest a portion, a nutrient value or a missing food, the suggestion (the food's name, barcode, amount and your comment) is saved in the app's public iCloud database, where we review it. The suggestion doesn't include your name.

Voice logging

If you speak food into the app, Apple's speech recognition is used. On devices that support it, recognition happens entirely on the phone; otherwise the audio is processed by Apple under Apple's terms. Repava never stores audio recordings — only the text you approve is logged.

Restaurants and location

If you allow it, the restaurant map uses your location to show restaurants near you. The location is only used while the map is open and is never stored. To find restaurants, the map's area is sent to Apple (Apple Maps and iCloud), never to us. Menus, nutrition values and allergens come from the restaurants themselves or from the chains' own nutrition information.

Notifications

Reminders are created and scheduled on the phone. News from Repava is sent via Apple: the phone saves a subscription in the app's public iCloud database, tied to an ID for your iCloud account that only applies to Repava, and Apple delivers the notification. It contains no information about you. Turn it off under Profile → Settings → Reminders → News from Repava.

Purchases

Purchases of Repava+ are handled by Apple. We never see or store payment details.

Third parties

Data is only shared with the services named above (Anthropic, Cloudflare, Open Food Facts, USDA and Apple), and only so the feature can work. We never sell data and don't use it for advertising. The services that receive personal data from Repava provide the same or equal protection for it as described in this policy.

Legal basis

Usage statistics, AI recipe interpretation and AI photo recognition are based on your consent, and health data on your explicit consent (GDPR Articles 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)). Other processing is needed to provide the app's features to you (Article 6(1)(b)). You can withdraw consent at any time in the app.

Retention and deletion

Data on the device remains until you delete it. Under Profile → Settings → Delete all data you remove everything Repava has stored on the phone; deleting the app does the same. Usage statistics are kept for at most one year. Delete all data also removes the statistics the phone has sent. If you want us to delete suggestions you've submitted, email repavainfo@vaao.se.

Your rights

You have the right to know what data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to or restrict processing. Contact us at repavainfo@vaao.se. You can also lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se.

Children

Repava is not intended for children under 13. If you are under 18, talk to an adult or a healthcare professional before trying to change your weight.

Changes

If the policy changes, we update this text and the date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email repavainfo@vaao.se.
