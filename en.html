Privacy Policy — NutriTrack

Updated July 6, 2026

NutriTrack does not collect any data about you. Everything you log — food, workouts, weight, sleep, recipes — is stored only locally on your iPhone. The app has no accounts, no analytics/tracking, no ads and no third-party SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Your profile (goals, body measurements, training and diet preferences), food diary, workouts, recipes, photos you attach to recipes, and sleep data are read/stored in the app's local database on your device. Delete the app and everything is deleted — we keep no copy.

Apple Health

With your explicit permission, the app reads workouts and sleep from Apple Health to display them in the app. Health data never leaves your device and is never used for marketing or shared with third parties.

Network requests

When you search for a food, the search term itself is sent to two open food databases (Open Food Facts, USDA FoodData Central) to fetch nutrition values; Livsmedelsverket's database is bundled in the app and searched locally. Searches carry no identity and are not linked to you. If you import a recipe, the app fetches the web page you yourself provide.

Voice logging

If you log food by voice, Apple's speech recognition is used. On devices that support it, recognition happens entirely on your device; otherwise the audio is processed by Apple under their terms. The app never stores audio recordings — only the text you approve is logged.

Purchases

NutriTrack+ purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see or store payment details.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date above will be updated.
